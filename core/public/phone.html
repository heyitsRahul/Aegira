Any phone is enough

Try it in 3 taps: press REG then SEND SMS; press SOS then SEND SMS; wait 30 seconds, or press CANCEL.

This simulates a basic phone talking to Aegira's SMS gateway — the channel for the hundreds of millions of phones that have no apps and no browser. Full guide · हिंदी

Commands: REG name contact pin pin, SOS, CANCEL pin, STATUS, SAFE pin, SEND (as a contact), STOP.

The tower selector stands in for the carrier's approximate network position — it enters the system only with an SOS, never with a registration.

Switch the number to the trusted contact's (9876543210) to receive the alert SMS and reply SEND.
