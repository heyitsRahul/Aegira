Privacy by architecture

Not a policy promise — a design guarantee. There is no table, cache, or log where your location exists without a live session.

🤫

Safe under coercion

A duress PIN returns a response identical to success while silently alerting your circle. The same protection covers journey check-ins.

📟

Reaches every phone

Smartphone app, a zero-JavaScript page for old browsers, and a full SMS channel for phones with no internet at all.

🧭

Coordination, not vigilantism

No public incident feed, no trust scores. Verified responders see only a session alias and a ~1 km area until they accept.

How it works

  1. Trigger — tap SOS, or arm a journey watch before you set out.
  2. Notify — your chosen trusted contacts are alerted with a live link.
  3. Match — a nearby verified responder is offered the session, identity-free.
  4. Verify & resolve — arrival is confirmed by a code; you mark yourself safe.
  5. Destroy — the per-session key is shredded; the data becomes unreadable.
