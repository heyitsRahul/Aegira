Only the right people know

Only your people and one trained helper are told. No public map, no crowd.

How it works

  1. Add one person you trust.
  2. In danger, press SOS (or text SOS).
  3. They get a message with where you are.
  4. If needed, a trained helper comes to you.
  5. When you are safe, everything is deleted.

Use it on any phone

📱

Smartphone

1. Open the app and choose your language.
2. Tap I need help and add one person you trust.
3. In danger, press SOS. You get 30 seconds to cancel.
Put it on your home screen: Android ⋮ → Install app. iPhone Share □↑ → Add to Home Screen.

🌐

Old phone browser

1. Open the simple version.
2. Register once with your number and two PINs.
3. In danger, press SEND SOS.

📟

Basic phone, no internet

Send REG your-name their-number pin pin once. In danger, send SOS. Try it here.
