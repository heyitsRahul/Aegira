That question kept bugging me while building Aegira.

Most safety apps have a cancel button. Great for false alarms. Not great if someone is standing right next to you.

So Aegira has two PINs.

Your normal PIN cancels.
Your secret PIN 𝘢𝘭𝘴𝘰 shows "cancelled".

But help keeps coming. Your family gets a secret alarm telling them not to call you.

The person next to you sees nothing different.
