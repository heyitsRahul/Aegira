Your Safety & Control

You are in control. If you need support, Aegira is designed to help you on your own terms. Nothing happens without your explicit consent. You decide who can see your location and exactly how long they can see it. The moment you choose to end a session, it is permanently closed. You never have to justify your situation, and you do not need to be certain of danger before you ask for help.

Overview

Help, on any phone, without becoming a surveillance system.

Aegira is a privacy-first emergency coordination platform. Instead of keeping a permanent log of where you are or who you contact, it limits location sharing and emergency coordination to a brief, temporary window.

Core Invariants

Four Design Guarantees

Aegira enforces your privacy through its core software architecture, not just a policy promise.

Location sharing is strictly temporary

Your location only exists while a session is active. It is never stored in database tables, browser caches, system logs, or background analytics.

session-shaped capability

Your data destroys itself when finished

When you end a session, the key needed to read your information is permanently destroyed. What the platform cannot decrypt, it can never leak, and it cannot be forced to share with third parties or legal authorities.

crypto-mortal data / crypto-shredding

Safe check-in and cancellation under pressure

If you are forced to cancel an alert or check in against your will, entering a specific secondary number returns a screen that looks identical to a normal success message, while silently notifying your contacts of the situation.

duress by design

Runs on any mobile hardware

You can access help on any phone. Aegira is available as a modern smartphone app, a lightweight web interface with zero scripts for older devices, or entirely over standard text messages without internet access.

runs on every phone
Architecture Rationale

Why this approach is necessary

Most safety and emergency coordination tools trade privacy for utility. They record and retain sensitive location histories in database records, temporary cache files, or analytics tracking. This builds up a permanent trail of your movement, exposing you to ongoing risks from security breaches, platform tracking, or external subpoenas. Many of these tools also require high-speed internet and modern smartphones, which means they do not work on basic feature phones or under poor network conditions.

Aegira is built to fix this. It ensures location information is locked inside active sessions, deletes details permanently using automated key destruction, and supports communication channels that run without internet connectivity.

Development Integrity

State of Development

System Warning

Aegira is currently a working prototype. The core session lifecycle, state machines, encryption, and protocols are real and validated through 282 tests. However, external delivery systems and third-party integrations are stubs (simulations). Do not rely on this prototype for active emergencies in its current state without configuring these connections.
